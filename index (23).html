What is a logical fallacy? 🤔

When people try to convince you of something, they make an argument. An argument has a claim (what they want you to believe) and reasons (why you should believe it).

A logical fallacy is a mistake in reasoning. It's a reason that sounds convincing, but it doesn't really prove the claim. Advertisers, social media posts, and even friends use fallacies all the time!

Should Our School Have a Four-Day Week?

1Our school should switch to a four-day school week. Students would have more time to rest and spend time with their families. In a survey of 300 families in our district, 210 said they would support a four-day week.

2Some people say a four-day week would be too hard for working parents. But the people who say this just don't care about students at all.

3Also, my friend's school switched to a four-day week last year, and her grades went up. This proves that a four-day week makes every student smarter.

4We only have two choices: switch to a four-day week, or watch every student burn out completely. The choice is clear.
